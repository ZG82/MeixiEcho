# Min Opera cultural prelude

The chapter-two opening uses archival images from Wikimedia Commons. The game
processes them into an original documentary-style sequence with cinematic camera
movement, cross-fades, captions, and original project audio. It is followed by an
in-game cultural quiz. No third-party performance video or audio is bundled.

Commercial and broadcaster-hosted Min Opera videos were reviewed for reference,
but were not redistributed because their pages did not grant a reusable license.
This keeps the GitHub and Windows releases independently redistributable.

## Cultural references

- China Intangible Cultural Heritage Digital Museum, "Min Opera":
  https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/13243.html
- Ministry of Culture and Tourism, first national intangible cultural heritage list:
  https://www.mct.gov.cn/whzx/ggtz/200606/t20060609_694679.htm
- Fujian Provincial People's Government, "National ICH: Min Opera":
  https://www.fujian.gov.cn/english/cultureandtravel/cultureandarts/202507/t20250722_6969826.htm

These references support the sequence and quiz facts: Min Opera is also called
Fuzhou Opera, uses the Fuzhou dialect for singing and dialogue, and was included
in the first national intangible cultural heritage list in 2006.
